# Design Prompts for Glosemester

Her er optimaliserte prompts du kan bruke i Midjourney, DALL-E 3 eller Adobe Firefly for å generere logo og header.

## 1. Logo
**Konsept:** Minimalistisk, akademisk men leken.
**Farger:** Sunset Orange (#FF9F64), Soft Lavender (#A5A6F6).

> **Prompt:**
> `Minimalist app logo for educational brand "Glosemester", abstract geometric shape combining a book and a star, rounded corners, soft vector style, energetic sunset orange and soft lavender color palette, white background, high quality, vector graphic, flat design, --no details, text`

## 2. Hovedheader (Hero Image)
**Konsept:** Inspirerende, ryddig, "studie-flow".
**Farger:** Soft Lavender gradient bakgrunn.

> **Prompt:**
> `Web header background for educational app, soft gradient background in soft lavender and pearl white, floating 3D minimalist icons of books, pencils, and trophy cups, trophy gold accents, high-radius soft shapes, clean, modern, energetic but focused, 8k resolution, wide aspect ratio --ar 16:9`

## 3. Ikoner (Fag)
**Konsept:** Lekne patch-lignende ikoner.

> **Prompt:**
> `Set of rounded minimalist icons for school subjects: mathematics, english, history. Sunset orange and trophy gold colors, vector sticker style, white border, flat design, playful and clean.`
